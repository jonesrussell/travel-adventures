Content and writing

  • Title: up to 160 characters. Default: Adventure · October 7, 2026, using creation date; editable and not unique.
  • Story: headings, bold, italics, lists and HTTP/HTTPS links; up to 20,000 visible-text characters.
  • Location: plain text, up to 200 characters.
  • Story and location can be unfinished in drafts; both required to publish.
  • Optional travel start/end dates. Future dates allowed privately; all supplied dates must be today or earlier to publish.
  • Media remains separate from story formatting.

Publishing and recovery

  • Private drafts or public adventures only.
  • Publish requires verified email and a chosen username, plus valid content and a preview confirmation.
  • Published edits appear immediately on explicit Save. Unpublish first for private editing.
  • Republishing retains original publication date and feed placement.
  • Owner-only trash lasts 30 days. Restore always returns a private draft.
  • Early permanent deletion from Trash is allowed after an irreversible-deletion warning and confirmation.

Author and visitor experience

  • My adventures replaces the dashboard: Drafts, Published, Trash; New adventure; most recently edited first.
  • Public feed replaces the welcome page: newest original publication first, 12 at a time, Load more.
  • Profile shows display name, username and published adventures only. Email stays private.
  • Username selected before first publish, fixed afterward: 3–30 lowercase letters, numbers or underscores; admin/support reserved.
  • Page links use stable ID plus title slug; old links redirect only after access checks.

Ownership and acceptance

  • A02: ownership assigned by server; create and reopen a draft.
  • A03: other accounts and guests cannot inspect drafts or trash.
  • A04: failed publishing validation never exposes content.
  • A05: feed and profiles exclude private/trashed adventures.
  • A06: only owner can edit, publish, restore or delete.
  • A10: accessible controls and honest Saving/Saved/failure feedback.
  • A11: JSON success and failure behavior matches the designed contract.

Agreed technical direction / not implemented

Technical choices selected

  • Story: Tiptap Vue 3, structured JSON in PostgreSQL; server validation and explicit safe Vue rendering.
  • Autosave: 1-second pause, one save in flight; integer version checks prevent overwriting another tab. Failed saves preserve local writing.
  • Publication: separate current visibility from original publication date. Transactions and row locks; repeated requests do not reset dates.
  • Trash: exact 30-day UTC deadline; hourly retry-safe cleanup. Restore and purge use row locks.
  • Dates: UTC defines today and generated-title creation date; travel dates remain date-only.
  • JSON: same-origin session and CSRF protection, versioned endpoints, explicit errors and cursor pagination.
  • Usernames: trim/lowercase, database uniqueness and reserved-name list; existing users choose before publishing.
  • Tests: Pest plus PostgreSQL integration. Playwright Chromium on PRs; Firefox/WebKit for staging.
