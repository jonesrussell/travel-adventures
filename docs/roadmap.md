# Roadmap

## 0. Product and specification foundation (current)

Deliver product brief, roadmap, architecture decisions and acceptance criteria. Identify open decisions. Preserve prototype history. No application implementation in this milestone.

## 1. Development foundation

Deliver Laravel/Vue scaffold, reproducible WSL Docker runtime, database, CI and verified Laravel Boost/Codex integration. Establish OpenAPI validation and contract testing workflow. Exit criteria: specs/001-foundation.md.

## 2. Core adventures

Deliver account authentication, drafts, publishing, paginated feed, adventure detail, profiles and author editing/deletion. Specify the OpenAPI contract before endpoints. Verify ownership and unpublished-content isolation.

## 3. Photos and reels

Deliver bounded uploads, private originals, photo rendering, queued video processing, poster images, playback and cleanup. Handle failed/retried jobs. This milestone is required for the MVP, not an optional later enhancement.

## 4. MVP release readiness

Deliver usable responsive flows, accessibility checks, error states, integration tests and a repeatable staging deployment. Demonstrate the end-to-end first-release acceptance criteria. Resolve privacy and operational decisions.

## 5. Public launch

Deliver reporting/moderation, privacy and account deletion policy, rate limiting, monitoring, verified backup restoration and deployment rollback. Choose hosting and budget before provisioning paid services.

## 6. Learn and expand

Use actual feedback to prioritize discovery/search, follows, reactions, saved adventures or planning. No commitment to build every social feature.

Each milestone follows specification, design, implementation, verification and review. Dates and effort estimates follow foundation discovery, not guesses.
