# Product brief

## Purpose

Help people share travel experiences as adventures combining a story, location, photos and short video reels. This is a real application; interview preparation is a secondary benefit.

## Confirmed requirements

- Fresh rebuild with the original prototype recoverable in Git.
- Laravel and Vue application.
- Photos and short video reels are first-class media.
- Spec Driven Development before implementation.
- OpenAPI contracts for the API.
- Laravel Boost as a priority development tool.

## Proposed starting scope

An author signs in, drafts an adventure, attaches media, previews it and publishes it. Visitors browse published adventures and author profiles. Authors edit or remove their own content.

Sharing is the working product direction. Trip planning, tracking, payments, messaging, maps and algorithmic recommendations are deferred.

## Decisions still open

- Intended initial audience and public launch scope.
- Whether launch supports public content only or additional visibility levels.
- Reel duration, upload limits, formats and storage provider.
- Hosting target, operating budget and moderation owner.

Resolve these before the relevant implementation milestone. Use explicit provisional choices for local development; do not silently turn them into business commitments.

## Success

A new user can publish a story with photos or a reel, another person can view it, and the author can safely edit or remove it. Operation, recovery and abuse handling must be demonstrable before public launch.
